Frequently Asked Questions

Can I really use the app for free?
Yes! Every new account gets 1 full business-class strategy prompt completely free. You'll experience the full power of Manager AI before you spend a kobo.
How does billing work?
Plans are billed monthly. You can pay by card (Paystack) or by bank transfer. Bank transfers are manually verified by our team and activated within a few hours after proof of payment.
What happens when my plan expires?
Your account automatically reverts to the free tier at midnight UTC on your expiry date. You won't be charged anything extra — you simply won't be able to generate new content until you renew.
I can't pay by card. What are my options?
No problem! Every plan has a "Pay via Bank Transfer" option. You'll transfer to our GTBank account, upload your receipt, and our team will activate your plan manually.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Yes! You can upgrade anytime. Contact us on Instagram @rae__hub to request a plan change and we'll sort you out.
